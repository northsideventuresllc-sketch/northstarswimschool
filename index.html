Our mission

Every kid deserves the water.

North-Stars Swim School is a community program of the North-Stars Foundation, built on a simple belief: swimming is a life skill, not a luxury. We're relaunching a proud local swim tradition with a people-first mission — safe water, strong athletes, and a place where every family belongs.

  • Water-safety education for the whole community
  • A competitive pathway that keeps kids in the sport
  • Access-first pricing and scholarships
  • Coaches who invest in people, not just times
